# Reconnaissance packet: book-of-songs

## Node contract

- Graph node: **The Book of Songs**, c. 1000 to 600 BCE, `easia`, ideas.
- Graph facts: the Shijing contains 305 poems, with voices associated with court, harvest, military service, labor, courtship, abandonment, and ritual. Tradition credits Confucius with selection, but the anthology was already a shared elite repertoire he quoted.
- Outgoing targets: `analects-of-confucius`, because Confucius treats the Odes as essential cultural training; `hundred-schools`, because later thinkers cite and reinterpret a common canon.
- Seed conflict: the poems are anonymous and not securely dated individually. “Folk songs” is a later classification, not proof that unedited villagers’ songs survive intact.

## Chronology

- The anthology’s poems span roughly the Western Zhou through the Spring and Autumn period, broadly the tenth to seventh centuries BCE, with individual dating uneven and often disputed.
- Confucius lived c. 551 to 479 BCE, later than much of the poetry. Do not portray him as the composer of the collection.
- The canonical label `jing` and the later institutional status of the Shijing belong to subsequent reception, especially Han-era canon formation, not necessarily to the poems’ first performance contexts.
- The Odes’ diplomatic use is best documented in the Spring and Autumn textual tradition, several centuries after the earliest poems but before the Han canonization.
- The anthology’s written witnesses are later than the composition window; oral performance, court transmission, and manuscript copying must remain distinct stages.

## Debates and traps

- Do not call the Shijing a simple anthology of “folk songs.” The `guofeng` category is a canonical classification, and court editors, performers, scribes, and later interpreters shaped what survives.
- Confucius’ traditional role is selection and moral transmission, not authorship. The collection predates him in large part.
- The poems’ speakers are personae. A first-person soldier, abandoned woman, farmer, or laborer is not a recoverable autobiography.
- Moral allegory was often imposed by later commentators. Preserve the tension between erotic, political, ritual, and didactic readings.
- “Diplomats quoted poems literally” is too strong. Verse citation could be indirect, allusive, strategic, and dependent on shared elite training.
- Canon formation was not a single ancient moment. Keep Western Zhou and Spring and Autumn performance, Warring States interpretation, and Han institutional canonization separate.
- Avoid treating the 305 poems as a transparent national voice. The collection is a curated archive of different regions, genres, and political functions.

## Scene evidence

- Real text object: 305 anonymous poems in four canonical divisions: 160 Airs of the States, 74 Minor Court Hymns, 31 Major Court Hymns, and 40 Eulogies.
- Material anchors: bamboo or wooden writing surfaces are plausible for early transmission, but the surviving canonical text is represented through later manuscript traditions and commentaries; do not invent a specific excavated manuscript for the whole collection.
- Performance evidence: short, rhythmically patterned lines, repeated images, refrains, and antiphonal or ceremonial possibilities; exact performance practice varies by poem and period.
- Social vocabulary: court ritual, harvest, war, conscription, marriage, separation, tribute, and seasonal labor.
- Human figures: a court performer, envoy, or teacher can be composite; an individual named author is not recoverable for most poems.
- Safe sensory evidence: memorized cadence, drums or bells in court ritual only when the selected poem and source support it; do not claim a universal instrument list.

## Open questions

- Exact dates and authorship for the poems selected by the writer remain unresolved.
- The full metadata for the Rutgers and Brill PDFs should be checked directly before a lesson cites them.
- The writer must choose one or two poems and verify the translation against the Chinese text; no English line should be treated as a transparent original voice.
- The precise mechanics of diplomatic citation belong to the Zuo Zhuan and other later texts, not automatically to the poems’ first performances.
